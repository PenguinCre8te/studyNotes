1.1 Major natural hazards

- Natural event

  - Caused by natural processes or forces out of human control

- Natural hazards

  - Natural event that cause harm and loss to humans

- Natural desasters

  - Great loss of life and severe property damage

- Major natural hazards

  - Geological

    - Volcanic eruption

    - Earthquake

    - Landslide

  - Meteorological hazard

    - Drought

    - Flood

    - Typhoon

  - Biological hazard

    - Pest (e.g. Plague of locusts)

1.2 Location of natural hazards

- Natural hazards caused by processes or forces occurring in different environments

- -> distrubution is related to the location of the environments where they form

- e.g. Floods are near rivers and coastal areas

- e.g. Typhoon mainly found in the tropical regions

- Few places are free of natural hazards

## 2. Relief

2.1 Showing relief

- Relief = shape of the land
  - Height
  - Steepness
- Showing relief
  - Colouring
  - Contouring

2.2 Cross section

- Draw a line on contour map and plot the changes in height on a line graph
- -> Cross section of land
- Shows shape and height of land

2.3 Describe steepness of slope

- Contour lines
  - Wide apart => Gentle slopes
  - Close together => steep slopes
- Slope gradient
  - $$= \frac {\text {vertical distance}} {\text {horizontal distance}} = \frac 1 x = 1 \text{ in } x$$ 
  - Larger => steeper

2.4 Relief features found on contour maps

- Steep slope

- Gentle slope

- Valley

- Spur
