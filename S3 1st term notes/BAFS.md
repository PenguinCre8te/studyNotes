### Accounting equation

- Assets = Liabilities + Capital

### Double Entry System

Rules

- Assets: left increase right decrease

- Liabilities: Left decrease right increase

- Capital: Left decrease right increase

### T-accounts

```
                        Title of account
 -------------------------------------------------------------
 Date | Details | Amounts ($) | Date | Details | Amounts
 ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^   ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
      Debit side (Dr.)              Credit side (Cr.)
```
