Fundementals of accounting

- Functions: Planning, Controlling

- Financial accounting: Information for external users -> Shows performance and position of business

- Management accounting: Information for internal users -> provide information for decision making

- Recording -> Classifying 

- -> Summarising (income statement, stmt of financial position) 

- -> Communicating (Extract and explain relavant information)

### Accounting equation

- Assets = Liabilities + Capital

### Double Entry System

Rules

- **Assets: Debit (left) increase**

- Liabilities: Left decrease right increase

- Capital: Left decrease right increase
