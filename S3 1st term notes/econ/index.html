What is Economics

  • Social science -> explain and predict human behaviour

  • Limited resources, unlimited wants

Microeconomics

  • Individual economics units (e.g. people, market. firms)

  • Make consumption and production

  • e.g. Demand and supply, Choices, Shortage and surplus

Macroeconomics

  • Performance of economic activities

  • Overall performance of economy

  • e.g. Inflation, GDP, unemployment

Wants

  • Human desires for things

  • Unlimited (always prefer more)

  • Can be physical or psychological

Scarcity

  • Resources < Wants

  • Limited resources, unlimited wants

Choices

  • Resource scarce, wants unlimited -> Make choices

  • e.g. deciding how to spend income

Scarcity -> Competition -> Discrimination

  • Price competition (e.g. Car, house)

  • Non-price competition (Concert ticket)

Opportunity cost

  • Scarcity -> Choice -> Opportunity cost

  • Cost of not choosing other options

  • = Highest-valued option forgone

Full cost

  • = Monetary cost (money cost) + Non-monetary cost (e.g. time)

Sunk cost

  • Cost paid in the past will NEVER be considered in opportunity cost

Interest

  • Cost of earlier avalibility of resources

  • Lender: compensation for deferring consumption

  • Borrower: price for obtaining good and resources earier

  • Exists in barter economies and one-man economies

  • In form of goods or money

  • Increase -> Current consumption decrease,
