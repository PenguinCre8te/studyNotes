[[Renaissance]] [[Scientific revolution]]
- A **intellectual and cultural movement** between the mid-17th and 18th centuries
- Stated that people enjoyed **==natural rights==** (equality, liberty)
- **opposed** political and social **inequalities**
Causes
- Change of value: Promoted idea of humanism -> applied to social and political aspects
- **==Discontent with absolutism==**
	- Absolute rulers promoted the '==**divine rights of kings**=='
	- -> Europeans began to **criticize** absolutism
Ideas
- ==**Laws of nature**==
	- Enjoyed inborn rights of equality and liberty
	- States and government were set up to protect
- ==**Social contract**==
	- About people and government
	- **==Locke==** + **==Rousseau==** suggested
		- **power of government came from people**
		- if government breaks contract and cannot protect people natural rights
		- people have the right to overthrow
- **==Separation of powers==** 
	- Montesquieu suggested
	- **==legislature==**, **==executive==** and **==judiciary==**
	- **prevent absolute rule**
- **==Constitution + Reforms==**
	- Voltaire suggested
	- **Limit the power of kings** and **protect people's rights** (freedom of speech, press, religion)
Spread
- **academic journals** became popular and was a important **mean** for Europeans to absorb Enlightenment ideas
- Publishers were eager to translate and print -> idea became **==widespread==**
Impact
- **==Liberating==** minds of European: Awakened Europeans to political system and pursuit to liberty
- ==**Spread**== in Europe and Americans -> American Revolution + French Revolution
- Enlightenment ideas -> Absolute rulers **gave more rights** (**Enlightened despots**)
