[[Renaissance]] Entering a new ==age of reason== - New understandings and views about nature, society and the world -> Scientific revolution and the Enlightenment - Modern times = age of faith: people accepted teaching of the Church - Renaissance: people challenged traditional teachings of the Church - Age of reason: scientist explained natural phenomena + proposed different nature laws

Scientific revolution¶

  • Scientific revolution: many new scientific theories were discovered Causes
  • ==Printing press== -> book production -> start of the scientific revolution
  • ==Voyages of discovery==: European navigators started a series of naval explorations 15-16 centuries
    • Discovered unknown lands (Americans)
    • Completed global circumnavigation
    • Widened the horizon of Europeans
    • Proved that earth was round Major developments
  • ==Scientific organizations==
    • Independent scientific bodies were founded
    • e.g. ==Royal Society== of London for Improving Natural Knowledge
    • Held meetings and published reports: provided a ==platform for academic exchanges==
  • Scientific methods and achievements
    • ==Kepler: Planetary motion: Kepler's Laws==
    • ==Harvey: Blood circulation in animals and humans==
    • ==Boyle: Principals behind natural phenomena, chemical elements==
    • ==Newton: Laws of universal gravitation== Impact
  • Stress on ==rational thinking== and a ==skeptical attitude==
  • ==Challenge== traditional authority
  • Laws of nature $\xrightarrow {inspired}$ the mindset of European thinkers
  • -> ==Applied the law to function of governments== -> Enlightenment
  • ==Application of scientific theories== -> Industrial revolution: developed ==new technologies + made inventions==
