[[Renaissance]] [[Scientific revolution]] - Technological advancement from 18th to 19th centuries - Key stage of the rise of modern Europe Characteristics - ==Mechanization==: Invented and used many new machines - ==Industrialization==: Experienced rapid growth and replaced traditional agriculture - ==Urbanization==: People moved from countryside to industrial towns Causes - ==Rise in population==: Increase demand for goods, provided labour - ==World trade==: to meet global demand - Accumulation of ==capital==: Invested on new machines + technological innovations - ==Development of science==: Applied technologies to improve industrial technologies Why start in Britain? - Rich in ==natural resources== (e.g. coal, iron) - Had many ==overseas colonies== - ==Enclosure movement==: Farmers lost their land -> move to cities and worked in factories - Government support - ==patent law== to protect rights of inventors - sponsored the ==Royal Society== to carry out scientific experiments Major developments - ==Textile industry== was first - Invention of ==steam engine== -> Spread to heavy industries (iron, steel making) - Made Britain rich - Spread to other European countries - Textile industry - Machine was operated by workers in factories - =='domestic system'== -> =='factory system'== - Spread to other sectors - Sources of energy - ==Steam power== - Watt improved the steam engine - ==Coal gas== - powering machines, gas lighting and cooking - ==Electricity== - 1870s Edison invented light bulb and first electric power system - ==Oil== - discovered in 19th century - used to power machine - Became the primary energy source - Other industries - Coal mining - Used steam power -> improved efficiency - Iron making - Before industrial revolution: Preferred cast iron bc cheap - Reduced cost of wrought iron and steel -> common industrial materials - Improvement in transportation - Before: Poor roads, used ox-cars, horse carts and sailing ships - Land transportation - ==Macadam road== - Better drainage - Smooth surface - 1883: First ==car== - Invented ==steam locomotives== and build ==railways== -> '==Age of railways==' - Became two major means of land transport - Sea transportation - ==Canals== were built - Invented and improved ==steamships== - Carry more further and faster - Communication - Before: by post and takes a long time - Invented ==telegraph==, ==Morse code== and telephone - Built ==telephone networks== - Medicine - Hygiene bad in slums - Started to study social medicines - Vaccines against ==smallpox==, ==cholera== and ==rabies== were invented - Discovered ==X-rays== and ==radium==: ==treat cancer== and observe bodies - Antiseptic surgery was used: prevent septicaemia by bacteria Impacts - Rise of ==capitalism== - Capitalists owned ==means of production== (machines, mines) - Put property into ==production== - Expanded through ==factory system== and ==joint-stock companies== - -> modern capitalism (or industrial capitalism) - Factory system - built factories, installed machines - Employed many workers - -> factory system - ==Division of labour== - Separated into specialized parts - More skilful in preforming their job - Save time and improve quality of work - Joint-stock companies - High cost -> joint-stock companies - Issued ==Stocks== and ==Bonds== to raise capital - Profit was distributed according to investment - Growth of industrial towns - Farmers want to get higher wages -> move to industrial towns - Large number of industrial town in Britain - + Urbanization - Rise of new social classes - ==Capitalist class== - industrialists, businessmen, owners of factories and mines - Rich - Can influence government policies - ==Middle class== - lawyers, doctors and engineers - ==Professional knowledge, expertise -> Wealth== - not as rich and influential - ==Working class== - ==Worked for capitalists== and earned little - Also called '==Proletariat==' - Labour problem - Low income: Woman and children had to work to earn extra - Long working hour: 14-15 hour per day - Frequent accidents: few factories or mines had safety measures - Poor living conditions: - Workers lived in crowded slums - Poor living + sanitary -> diseases - Labour movements - ==Trade Union==: improve ==conditions and wages== - British recognised the legal status (1824) - Trade unions organised '==Chartist Movement==' to demand - shorter working hours and higher wages - Right to vote for every man over 21 - Representative to take part in government - Government passed acts to improve workers' conditions - Disallow Children and woman to work - Limit working hours - But other country didn't improve until 20th century - ==Socialism== - Origin - thinkers criticized ills of capitalism - Private ownership -> few capitalists control means of production - ==Exploit working class for profit== - Means of production should be ==owned publicly== - ==Wealth== should be ==distributed evenly== - -> 'Socialism' - Development - Representatives: British Owen's, French Saint-Simon's, 'utopian socialism', German Engels', Marx's 'communism' - Socialism spread to many countries - Marxism gained the greatest attention - Communist theories and movements affected the world - ==Expansion== of world trade - Western industrial powers imported large quantities of raw materials from different places - Improvement of transportation -> rapid development of international trade and technological exchange - Economic and colonial expansion - Industrial revolution -> more power - -> Acquire more raw materials and overseas markets - Speed up economic and colonial expansion in Asia and Africa - -> New imperialism (extending power through colonization) - Set up many colonies - International economic system developed - Colonial disputes and competition -> conflicts among western powers - -> ==tense international situation==
