[[The Renaissance]]

What is it¶

  • cultural and intellectual movement that took place in Europe
  • 14th to 17th centuries
  • Started in italy
  • Renaissance = revival, rebirth
  • refers to the revival of the anicnet greco-roman civilization
  • Innovated + carried forward the classical civilization
  • laid the foundation of modern Europe
  • transitional period from medieval times to modern times

Features¶

Revival - revive of the anicnet greco-roman civilization - Studied and applied theories and art styles to their works Humanism - X God-centered - Concerned with Worldly Affairs - Humans = center of the universe - Have independent thinking - Develop capacities - = Humanism Challenging tradition - Stressed the sprit of inquiry and humanism - Challenged traditional idea and church teachings - Scientific discoveries and inventions were made

Causes of the Renaissance¶

Decline of feudal society - peasants left manors and lived in towns - -> freeman -> greater freedom and time for art and learning - -> Renaissance Spread of eastern knowledge - Arab know more than European - Crusades (11-13 centries) encouraged knowledge exchange between Europe and Arab world - Brought legacies of ancient civilizations - Borden horizon + Revived intrest in Greco-Roman art and learning Rise of wealthy city states - Ports and towns developed rapidly - Italian city-states became major trading centers between Europe and Asia Rise of wealthy class - Wealthy personal emerged in Italy and other European countries - Concerned with worldly life - Had money to support art and science Rise of universities - Goverment / merchants built universities since the 12 century - Education not only controlled by church - Universities taught subjects other than bible - e.g. medicine law phyilosophy - Kept bookes about ancinet Greco-Roman civilization - -> people find more abt it Deline of church influence - Gained knowledge -> questioned the teachings of the church - Weakened the influence of the church - Crusades fail -> undermined the prestiege of the Church - Rulers refused to pay tax and defied the pope

Birthplace and developement¶

Birthplace¶
  • Started on the Italian Penisula (also the birthplace of ancient Roman civilization)
  • Preserved many Roman legacies
  • Had rich city-states
  • Welthy supported the work of artists and scholars
Literature¶
  • Literature Before Renassance
  • related to religon (eg biographs of saints, prayers and hymns) + written in Latin
  • Only preists and scholars can read and compose
  • Literature After Renassance: Wrote about people, affairs and things
  • wrote poems, novels and plays
  • Many wrote in their native language
  • Literature -> popular Poems
  • Representative: Dante
  • Wrote Divine Comedy in latin about traveling to heaven and hell -> social inequality Novels
  • Representative: Boccaccio
  • Wrote Decaneron about young people taking about wrongdoings of church and man Plays
  • Representative: Shakespeare
  • The most representative playwright
  • wrote many famous plays in english exploring different aspects of human life
Art¶

Paintings - Medieval time - Mainly about religon - Not realistic - Renassance - Had diverse themes - more 3d and have perspective and shadowing - Studied human body to paint lifelike human body and facial expressions - Painters - Leonardo da vinci - Use perspective - e.g. mona lisa, last supper - Michelanngelo - Detalied body movements and facial expressions - e.g. Last judgements - Raphael - harmony, joy, happiness - e.g. School of Athens - Known as three master artists Sculptures - Medieval - Religon - Renassance - Still from bible - but more facial expression and body movement      <- humanism - famous for realism Architecture - Medieval - Gothic (German) style - Tall pointed towers, Pointed Arches , flying buttresses and stained glass - Renassance - stone pillars, pediments, semi-circular arches and domes - Colonnades(rows of colloms) were built around plaza Natural science development - Before Renassance - Church translated many ancient science classic and intergrated them with theory to explain the universe - e.g. geocentrism (earth center) - claimed diseases were God's punishments - prohibited dissecion of the 'scared' human body - During renassance - scholars studied the original copies - found difference form church translation - Verified theories with observation and research - known as 'scientific enquiry' - Discoveries in Medicine - Fracastoro: diseases ware spread by bacteria (bread on decayed matter) - Vesalius: Dissected human bodies, described human skeleton, organs and muscles in his book - Discoveries in astronomy - Copernicus: Proposed the theory of heliocentrism -> sun (helio) is the center of the universe - Galileo: made telescopes to observe the universe + carried out experiments and affirmed heliocentrism

Influence of the Renaissance¶

Laying out foundations for modern Europe - Renaissance: revived the ancient Greco-Roman civilization - made great achievement and developed rapidly - laid the Foundation for moderm European civilization Rise of humanistic education - Aim of education is not clergy(e.g. pope) but 'renassance man' - people went to non church school to get worldly knowledge - -> develop European education and culture Intellectual liberation (the process of freeing the mind) - Renassance freed teh minds of Europeans - NO longer obeyed traditional authority blindly - valued rational thinking - led to Reformation, voyages of discovery, the scientific revolution and the elightenment - milestones in the rise of modern europe, starting in Renassance
