[[The Renaissance]]
#### What is it
- <mark>cultural and intellectual</mark> movement that took place in Europe
- **14th to 17th** centuries
- Started in **<mark>italy</mark>**
- **Renaissance = <mark>revival, rebirth</mark>**
- refers to the **<mark>revival of the anicnet greco-roman civilization</mark>**
- Innovated + carried forward the classical civilization
- laid the foundation of modern Europe
- **transitional period** from medieval times to modern times
#### Features
Revival
- revive of the <mark>anicnet greco-roman</mark> civilization
- Studied and applied **theories and art styles** to their works
**Humanism**
- X God-centered
- Concerned with **Worldly Affairs**
- Humans = center of the universe
- Have independent thinking
- Develop capacities
- = <mark>**Humanism**</mark>
Challenging tradition
- Stressed the sprit of **inquiry** and **humanism**
- **Challenged** <mark>traditional idea</mark> and <mark>church teachings</mark>
- Scientific **discoveries and inventions** were made
#### *Causes* of the Renaissance
Decline of feudal society
- peasants left manors and lived in towns
- -> freeman -> greater <mark>freedom</mark> and time for art and learning
- -> <mark>Renaissance</mark>
Spread of eastern knowledge
- <mark>Arab</mark> know more than European
- <mark>Crusades</mark> (11-13 centries) encouraged knowledge exchange between Europe and Arab world
- Brought <mark>legacies</mark> of ancient civilizations
- Borden horizon + **Revived intrest** in Greco-Roman art and learning
Rise of wealthy city states
- Ports and towns developed rapidly
- Italian city-states became <mark>major trading centers</mark> between Europe and Asia
Rise of wealthy class
- Wealthy personal emerged in Italy and other European countries
- Concerned with <mark>worldly life</mark>
- Had money to <mark>support art and science</mark>
Rise of universities
- Goverment / merchants built <mark>universities</mark> since the 12 century
- Education **not** only controlled by church
- Universities taught subjects other than bible
- e.g. medicine law phyilosophy
- Kept **bookes about ancinet Greco-Roman civilization**
- -> people find more abt it
Deline of church influence
- Gained knowledge -> questioned the teachings of the church
- Weakened the influence of the church
- Crusades fail -> undermined the prestiege of the Church
- Rulers refused to pay tax and defied the pope
