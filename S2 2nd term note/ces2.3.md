#### Public finance

- -> <mark>**government finance**</mark> (revenue / expenditure)

- for **public services**

- to **improve citizens' quality of life**, **maintain social stability** and promote **sustainable** development

- **Revenues**
  
  - <mark>**Non-tax revenue**</mark>
    
    - <mark>**Investment income**</mark>
    
    - **Land Premium**: selling land
    
    - **Fees / Charges / Utilities**
  
  - <mark>**tax revenue**</mark>
    
    - <mark>**Salaries tax**</mark>
    
    - **Stamp duties**: when buying, selling, or transferring property
    
    - **Profits tax**: on profits obtained by business operations

- **Expenditure**
  
  - **Health**
  
  - **Social welfare**
  
  - Education
  
  - Economic (promote tourism)
  
  - Housing

- **Tax system** of Hong Kong
  
  - **Fewer tax items** -> **simpler tax system**
    
    - **no sales tax**
  
  - **Lower tax rate**
  
  - **Narrow tax base** (number of **tax items**, number of **taxpayers**)

#### Public finance

**the Budget**

- sets out the **estimate** of government revenue and expenditure

**Objectives**

- <mark>**Income redistribution**</mark> 
  
  - -> Public services, Financial support

- <mark>**Promoting economic development**</mark>
  
  - Reducing tax
  
  - Supporting industries
  
  - Investing in education
  
  - Attracting overseas professionals
  
  - Improving infrastructure

- **Responding to social needs**
  
  - <mark>**Poverty alleviation**</mark>
    
    - Recurring cash items: long term, e.g. Old Age Living Allowance
    
    - Non-recurring cash items: one off
    
    - Non-cash items: Public housing
    
    - have to fulfill specified criteria
  
  - <mark>**Housing**</mark>
    
    - Increase land supply
    
    - X inadequate housing(subsidised units, caged home)
  
  - <mark>**Aging population**</mark>
    
    - More healthcare
    
    - Less labour less tax
    
    - -> Finance worsens

#### Requirements of public finance

- Comply with basic law
  
  - achieve **fiscal balance**
  
  - avoid deficit
  
  - budget correspond to growth of GDP

- Scenarios
  
  - **Surplus budget**
  
  - **Balanced budget**
  
  - **Deficit budget**

- **In accordance of policies**
  
  - e.g. provide support for small and medium businesses -> Inject 1.5b find to support

- Record of government
  
  - Generally **maintain a fiscal balance**
  
  - Often affected by economic environment / unexpected events -> deficit budget
  
  - Increasing fiscal reserves
  
  - stable % of GDP
