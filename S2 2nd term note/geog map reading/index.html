Grid references¶

We locate object by using four-figure grid reference

  • combine Easting and Northing

  • And six figure grid reference :

  • E.g.for the trigonometric station, it is on 069619

  • Use a ruler to measure

  • South -> Southern hemisphere

Relief¶
  • Relief = shape of land, height+steepness

Ways of showing

  • Colouring

  • Contouring

  • on Contour map

  • in Contour lines

  • -> Height and Shape of the land + steepness of slopes

  • By conventional signs

  • trigonometric stations

  • spot heights

  • Marks the height at a certain place

Figuring height of place

  • Know the Vertical Interval: Difference in value between two adjacent contour lines

  • On contour line: Same value as contour line

  • Between contour lines: Guess

  • Above all contour lines: $\text{last ccontourline} + \frac {\text{Vertical Interval}}{2}$

Vertical exaggeration (V.E.)

  • For cross section
  • Show shape of a land more clearly -> Larger vertical scale
  • Vertical exaggeration = enlargement of scale of vertical scale
  • = $\frac {\text{Vertical scale}} {\text{Horizontal scale}}$
Slope n Gradient¶
  • Studying spacing of the contour lines or calculating the slope gradient
  • -> Steep slope or gentle slope

Studying spacing of contour lines

  • Closely packed -> Steep slope
  • Wide apart -> gentle slope

Calculating slope gradient

  • Gradient -> how steep the slope is
  • Rise over run
  • in forms of 1 in 10 or 1:10 -> for every 10 units along the ground, the land rises one unit
  • Larger -> steeper slope
  • \text{Gradient} = \frac {\text {Vertical distance}} {\text {Horizontal distance}}
  • Vertical distance: Read contour lines
  • Horizontal distance: Measure with ruler, multiply by map scale

Relief features found on contour maps

  • Concave slope 凸

  • steep at upper part, gentle at lower part

  • gradient decreases downslope

  • Convex slope 凹

  • gentle at upper part, steep at lower part

  • -> Gradient increases downslope

  • Cliff

  • Vertical or near vertical features -> Very closely packed together

  • If cliff vertical contour lines will touch each other to form line or point

  • Spur 凸

  • Long, sloping land that runs down

  • Usually found between valleys

  • Contour bends towards higher ground

  • Valley 凹

  • Long stretch of low-lying land between higher land or spurs

  • Often has a river running through it

  • Contour bends towards lower ground

  • Hill

  • Area of higher ground

  • Shown by concentric circle

  • Types of hills

    • Round-topped hill
    • Conical hill
    • Flat-topped hill

  • Ridge

  • Long narrow range of higher ground

  • Chain of mountain or hills

  • Contour lines are usually close and elongated (窄長的)

  • Saddle(鞍部)

  • lowest point along a ridge connecting two peaks

  • Shallow gap on a mountain ridge between two peaks

  • Looks like a saddle on a horse

  • Pass(峽道)

  • Deep and narrow gap in a mountain range

  • Roads are often built through for easy travelling

  • Plateau

  • Large area of flat land at higher ground

  • Flat area surrounded by sloping land

  • River flow outwards

  • Basin

  • Piece of flat land with all side surrounded by higher ground

  • Rivers flow inward
