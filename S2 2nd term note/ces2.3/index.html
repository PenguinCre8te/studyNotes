Public finance¶

  • -> government finance (revenue / expenditure)

  • for public services

  • to improve citizens' quality of life, maintain social stability and promote sustainable development

  • Revenues

  • Non-tax revenue

    • Investment income

    • Land Premium: selling land

    • Fees / Charges / Utilities

  • tax revenue

    • Salaries tax

    • Stamp duties: when buying, selling, or transferring property

    • Profits tax: on profits obtained by business operations

  • Expenditure

  • Health

  • Social welfare

  • Education

  • Economic (promote tourism)

  • Housing

  • Tax system of Hong Kong

  • Fewer tax items -> simpler tax system

    • no sales tax

  • Lower tax rate

  • Narrow tax base (number of tax items, number of taxpayers)

Public finance¶

the Budget

  • sets out the estimate of government revenue and expenditure

Objectives

  • Income redistribution

  • -> Public services, Financial support

  • Promoting economic development

  • Reducing tax

  • Supporting industries

  • Investing in education

  • Attracting overseas professionals

  • Improving infrastructure

  • Responding to social needs

  • Poverty alleviation

    • Recurring cash items: long term, e.g. Old Age Living Allowance

    • Non-recurring cash items: one off

    • Non-cash items: Public housing

    • have to fulfill specified criteria

  • Housing

    • Increase land supply

    • X inadequate housing(subsidised units, caged home)

  • Aging population

    • More healthcare

    • Less labour less tax

    • -> Finance worsens

Requirements of public finance¶

  • Comply with basic law

  • achieve fiscal balance

  • avoid deficit

  • budget correspond to growth of GDP

  • Scenarios

  • Surplus budget

  • Balanced budget

  • Deficit budget

  • In accordance of policies

  • e.g. provide support for small and medium businesses -> Inject 1.5b find to support

  • Record of government

  • Generally maintain a fiscal balance

  • Often affected by economic environment / unexpected events -> deficit budget

  • Increasing fiscal reserves

  • stable % of GDP

Relationship between public finance and citizens¶

  • Obligation to pay taxes and rights to social welfare

Showing concern for social affairs

  • Citizens should

  • Care about society

  • Understand the needs of different classes and groups

  • Express view on budgets

    • Government launches public consultation on the Budget

    • Different channels

    • Website

    • Phone

    • Fax

    • Post

    • Email

    • Social media platforms

Why?

  • Ensure policies are in line with public interest

  • Enhancing public concern for social affairs

  • Increasing transparency in governance

Analysing public finance

  • from the overall well-being of society

  • provide reasonable suggestions

  • Perspectives

  • Society

    • Can it meet the needs of public

    • Can it solve social problems

  • Economy

    • e.g. How will it affect the economy (GDP/consumption/investment/unemployment/inflation)

  • Financial resources

    • e.g. Do government have sufficient resource
